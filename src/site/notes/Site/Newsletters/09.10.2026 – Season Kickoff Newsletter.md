# Major Changes

Here I'm going to enumerate the various changes that have been made, either deliberately or due to things like needing to handle 9-team leagues to keep things balanced (and make the Grand Champion system still make sense, which a 10- and 8-team league setup would break).

## The Mean Team

As you'll all recall, I mentioned on draft day that I'd figure out how to handle 9-team leagues to account for our 18 participants this year. Well, as this is new territory for me, I gave it my best shot and hope it works well and fairly, lol.

My solution to the problem is that I've added a dummy team that I've dubbed **The Mean Team,** which is a clue as to how it scores points:

> [!important] **The Mean Team's Scoring**
> The Mean Team's score is computed as a **trimmed mean (average)** of all real teams in the league, except for the highest and the lowest scoring teams. This is to prevent outlier high-scoring and low-scoring teams from skewing **The Mean Team's** score.

If you recall https://app.pvffl.com, the website I built to handle my custom playoff seeding, I've updated it to also handle this season's dummy team computation. Here are the current matches in the North league:

![Pasted image 20260910152128.png](/img/user/z_Assets/Pasted%20image%2020260910152128.png)

You'll note that **The Mean Team** has a score of 7.96. Here's the breakdown for that:
```
22.20 and one of the 0.00 scores are discarded, as they are the highest and the lowest scores.

Sum the remaining:
  13.00
+ 12.82
+ 12.00
+ 11.10
+  6.80
+  0.00
+  0.00
-------
= 55.72
/  7    (number of summed scores)
-------
=  7.96 (The Mean Team's score)
```

For all intents and purposes, **The Mean Team** is like any other team in the league: you will have one or two matchups against it, and those will impact your league record. Some key things to know:
- This team obviously cannot enter the playoffs. It is excluded from playoff seeding.
- It is not included in League Median calculations, so now for the first time, LM ties are possible and have been handled in the web app.

### Playoff Seeding

Related to all that, with 9 real teams in the league, I've set this season's number of playoff teams to **five.** That's:
1. Top team by record (Round 1 BYE)
2. Second team by record (Round 1 BYE)
3. Third team by record (Round 1 BYE)
4. Fourth team by record
5. Among remaining five teams, the team with the best League Median record. (Wildcard)

So four teams will miss the cut.

## FAAB Has Arrived

Free Agent Acquisition Budgets have been introduced to the PVFFL, something I've refrained from doing for the past several seasons. Like a frog in a pot of boiling water, I've very slowly and deliberately conformed the PVFFL ~~into my image~~ into what I prefer to commish/play in. 
So far:
- ESPN => Not ESPN (i.e. Sleeper)
- Standard scoring => Half-PPR
- Small trophy with no sense of legacy => Absolutely ludicrous trophy with winners' names etched for all eternity
- Default playoff seeding => Wildcard seeding using the [[Site/Misc/PVFFL League Constitution#Section 3 – The League Median\|League Median]]
- Default waiver schedule => Custom daily waivers
- And now, Rolling waiver priority => FAAB bidding

To break it down, I'll describe what we had and what we have now.

**What we had:** Rolling waiver priority. Teams started with a priority in inverse draft order (i.e. the team with pick 1 had last priority, team with last pick had first). This priority was used to determine which team got a player if both teams claimed them. 
So if Team 1 and Team 2 both claimed Bijan Robinson off waivers, the team with the higher priority got them. Then, the team that got the player would lose priority and drop to the bottom of the list. Meaning the next week, if they claimed a player that another team wanted, they would likely lose the tiebreaker.

**What we have:** Free Agent Acquisition Budgets. Every team starts the season with $100 of Monopoly money to spend on waiver wire players. That is your budget **for the whole season.** When claiming a player on the wire, you secretly specify how much of your budget you're willing to fork up for that player. When waivers clear, if you bid the most, you get the player! But if you were outbid, you would not.
In the case of a tie, where two teams bid the same amount, the system still uses rolling waiver priority under the hood to break them.

I vastly prefer FAAB to any other waiver system, since it equalizes the process. You're no longer punished for being an active waiver participant, and any team has a shot for a given player if they want them badly enough and have the cash.

**FAQ**
- **How do I get more money?** The only way to get more is by trading for it from other teams.
- **Do I have to bid a nonzero amount?** Nope! You can bid $0 and get the player provided no one outbids you.

## Section 2 – Daily Waivers

To keep things fair, since different owners have different schedules, PVFFL leagues use daily waivers throughout the season on the following schedule:
- Monday – Waivers[^2]
- Tuesday – Waivers[^2]
- Wednesday – Waivers
- Thursday – Waivers to Free Agency[^3]
- Friday – Waivers
- Saturday – Free Agency
- Sunday – Free Agency

Waivers clear every day at 3am US/Eastern when applicable.

[^2]: Players stay on waivers after games until Wednesday at 3am.
[^3]: After waivers clear on Thursday, players go on FA to allow owners to make instant claims in time for Thursday Night Football.


> [!Question] Why?
> Midweek news/injury reports trickle in throughout the week, and owners who are more able to instantly react to news by claiming players when news breaks have a distinct advantage over owners who are less connected during the work day. Daily waivers ensures that all owners have an equal shot at claiming players that are suddenly valuable.
