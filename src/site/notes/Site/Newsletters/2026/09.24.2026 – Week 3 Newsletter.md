**The North**
In the North, we have both the top and bottom scorers on the week across both leagues. The top scorer was [[Site/Teams/@BrianH\|@BrianH]] (more on this team way down below) at **161.50.** The bottom scorer across both leagues was [[Site/Teams/@OliviaH (Mrs. Fluff & Mini Mallow)\|@OliviaH (Mrs. Fluff & Mini Mallow)]], who fell 35.14 points shy of a perfect lineup at **87.24** points. **The South**
The top scorer in the South was [[Site/Teams/@OneTrustyGuy 🏆\|@OneTrustyGuy 🏆]] at **159.22**. The bottom scorer was [[Site/Teams/@AlexW\|@AlexW]] at **88.24,** exactly one point higher than [[Site/Teams/@OliviaH (Mrs. Fluff & Mini Mallow)\|@OliviaH (Mrs. Fluff & Mini Mallow)]]'s performance and barely cleared the worst-scorer-across-both-leagues bar. [[Site/Teams/@Scottsteam2 (Colts>Eagles&Lions)\|@Scottsteam2 (Colts>Eagles&Lions)]] had a nice bounce-back week at **155.06** points after a poor Week 1 showing.

The South, notably, is undoubtedly stronger across the board than the North through two weeks. The rest of the newsletter will dig into that more, but it isn't just a matter of more evenly spread strength/points (the top and bottom scorers in the South are about the same as in the North points-wise). Across most positions, South teams score just a bit more on average than North teams. Which is a perfect segue into...
