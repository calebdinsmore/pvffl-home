> [!info]- How the numbers were made
> 200,000 simulations per league (seed 7); Sleeper data pulled Monday Nov 9, 4:18 PM.
> Each remaining starter is drawn from their own game log (2025 and 2026 so far, scored with our settings; only games they actually played, at least 25% of snaps for offensive players), smoothed, and recentered so 60% of its mean comes from this week's Sleeper projection. Under 6 games it's the projection times a position-level actual÷projection ratio; between 6 and 12 games it's a mix.
> Players in the same NFL game move together the way real ones have: the rank correlations of each position pair's scores against projection, teammates and opponents separately, measured over 2021 to 2025 (a QB and his own WRs about +0.33, his TE +0.27, the two QBs +0.21, a DEF and the QB it faces -0.39). Each player's own range of outcomes is unchanged; only which outcomes land in the same simulation is.
> The Mean Team's score is recomputed inside every simulation (drop the league's highest and lowest, average the rest), and so is the League Median.
> The past-game columns ("X of last Y") count the player's own games that reached the bar (or, in a flipped table, stayed under it), over their last 18 games and over every game Sleeper has back to 2014 (played games only, same snap floor, scored with our settings). It is context only; the simulation uses the two most recent seasons.
> When the underdog has no one left, the table flips to the favorite's players: "Under" is how often each one stays below the underdog's lead on their own (a must, since any one of them clearing it ends the miracle), in the simulations and in their past games.
> "Needs" is what a player must score for their own team to win if every other remaining player on both sides lands exactly on their median; "Odds" is how often they clear it.
> Playoff stakes: the Playoff Picture model plays out the rest of the season 100,000 times, each starting from one of tonight's simulated finishes (so every season has a result for every team tonight), with each team's current roster and Sleeper projections. A team's odds after a result are the share of seasons with that result in which it made the playoffs or got a bye; the rooting guide compares a team's odds when another matchup goes one way vs the other (only matchups whose underdog has at least a 3% chance).
