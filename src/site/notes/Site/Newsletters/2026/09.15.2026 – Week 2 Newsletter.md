# Week 1 Recap

The North league saw both our highest and lowest scoring teams across both leagues:
- I, [[Site/Teams/@CommissionerSmore (Bijan Kenobi)\|@CommissionerSmore (Bijan Kenobi)]], scored **170.46** in my matchup against [[Site/Teams/@Treg2025 (Team Treg)\|@Treg2025 (Team Treg)]], who left the most points on the bench across both leagues (53.10 short of a perfect lineup). A perfect lineup would have taken the win, but that's a tall order.
- My named top contender in the North, [[Site/Teams/@Kathy8 (AJ's Book Club)\|@Kathy8 (AJ's Book Club)]], came out the gate face-first with a brutal sub-100 performance. Scoring **97.50** points, Kathy lost to [[Site/Teams/@cardkid2331 (It's Gonna Be Maye)\|@cardkid2331 (It's Gonna Be Maye)]] but also could have nabbed a W with a perfect lineup.

The South saw some Monday-night excitement, with [[Site/Teams/@cpence0712 (Gibbsmethetrophy)\|@cpence0712 (Gibbsmethetrophy)]] getting an unlikely win by **2.66** points thanks to an exceptional performance by **Kenneth Walker**, who scored a whopping **32.60** points.
Prior to Monday's matches I was going to do a short Monday-night miracles newsletter (look out for those in the future) but didn't pull it together. What I did pull together, however, was simulations of what teams needed to pull out unlikely Monday-night wins, which included this graphic:

In other South news, [[Site/Teams/@blamie\|@blamie]] got the top score with **158.76**, with [[Site/Teams/@Scottsteam2 (Colts>Eagles&Lions)\|@Scottsteam2 (Colts>Eagles&Lions)]] bringing up the rear at **107.66**.
